# Arena fighting game

I wanna create a small arena fighter for me and my friends, small in the sense that the base content is not really deep but we would be able to edit the game, add maps, add weapons / equipment and generally extend the playing experience

## inspiration

- superfighters is a great game, they have good movement, ladders, windows that break, good weapons and fun maps, they have if i remeber correctly destroyable furniture and exploding stuff
- duck game is a similar game, more chaotic and if i remember coreectly with some destroyable terrain, i've not played that one as much but it was fun
- stick man fighter, a steam game i played a little with my friends but felt a little impactless
- Broforce, great character game where you can play a story or co op, destroyable terrain, great loadouts and powerups and gore

there are probably hundreds of games with the same premise, i wanna create one thaat has the following

## features

- pixel artstyle
- destroyable terrain
- smooth and satisfying movement, superfighters has sprinting, diving (jumping when yuou are sprinting)
- good unarmed combat, make jumping and attacking do something different than just attacking, in superfighters we can tackle people by diving into them and disarm them by punching them while they aim at us
- good armed combat, i'm torn between having a keyboard + mouse or just keyboard, the aiming in superfighters is good but maybe even overkill
- different equipment that changes the gameplay, i had some ideas there

## items

### equipment

- gravity suit like from gravity guy, would flip the screen for the equipped player
- a jetpack (can be upgraded by collecting a minigun, jetpack joyride style)
- grappling hooks (can catch people or corners of walls and swing)
- webshooters (spiderman style, similar to grappling hook but pulls the equipped player in and enemy players get stuck or slowed)

- minigun

### melee

(most of them improve movement somewhat)

## bombs
